How Shield would work

The team's selected idea: an AI agent assigned to each new hire that gives correct security info fast and checks a suspicious email or link before it is opened. The three games are where people meet it.

What Shield does (the same four steps in every game)
  1. Nudge before: a risky click on a flagged item is paused: "Hold on, the sender is northbr1dge.co, not northbridge.co. Still open it?"
  2. Explain after: one line naming the red flag and the safe habit.
  3. Personalize: it tracks misses across 8 categories (urgency, look-alike domain, credential ask, gift card or payment, attachment, MFA fatigue, oversharing, device hygiene) and nudges sooner where a person keeps missing.
  4. Debrief: each player's two weakest categories with a tip, and how many mistakes Shield stopped.
Where a real AI model plugs in
📩 Message arrives (email, chat, text, a "call from IT" note)
🧠 Shield.assess(text): today simple rules on this page; in a real version a language model plus the company's allowlist of real domains and threat feeds
🚦 Traffic light + reasons + the safe next step
🚩 "Report to IT" in one tap; the miss or the save is logged for the person's own coaching
Guardrails the team would keep
  • It advises, it never blocks work: the person always decides.
  • Coaching data stays with the person; team reports show totals, not names.
  • It says when it is unsure ("careful") instead of guessing "safe".
  • Real reports go to the security team, who stay the decider.
